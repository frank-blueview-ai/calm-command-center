## Short “how we built it” note

```text
We built a lightweight web app around a calm-first safety protocol:
1) Voice/text intake of high-stress message,
2) GPT-5.5 structured de-escalation analysis (facts, assumptions, risks, response options),
3) Send-safe final response generation,
4) Image Gen brief card for clear downstream communication,
5) NeutrinoRTC-style live voice turns with barge-in interruption so users can redirect mid-response.

Design principles were Blue View DNA: calm under pressure, human-first, ethical clarity, and practical action.
```

## 60-second live demo script

### 0-10s
“Here’s Calm Command Center. Paste any heated message, or use live voice turn.”

### 10-25s
Paste sample message:
> “This is unacceptable. You dropped the ball again. Fix it now.”

Click **Analyze**.

### 25-40s
Call out output quickly:
- emotional temperature,
- facts vs assumptions,
- 3 response styles,
- send-safe reply.

Say: “This prevents reactive replies and protects the relationship.”

### 40-50s
Click **Generate Image Card**.

Say: “Now we instantly produce a shareable brief card for alignment.”

### 50-60s
Click **Live Voice Turn (Barge-in)** while assistant is speaking.

Say: “NeutrinoRTC-style barge-in lets me interrupt and redirect in real time.”

Close with:
“From chaos to calm, safely, in under a minute.”
